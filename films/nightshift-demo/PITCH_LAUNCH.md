## Draft spoken pitch

When a report job stops, some of its work may already be complete. The next action depends on the evidence: the worker lease, saved checkpoint, original input and errors.

Nightshift is an AI agent that investigates that state, selects an allowlisted repair, and checks the deliverable. The model chooses tools; code enforces permissions, lease generations and request limits.

In this controlled Sonnet run, the worker lease had expired and parsing was saved. Nightshift reclaimed the observed generation, resumed from the checkpoint, and reconciled the report against the frozen input. The result was five rows, one hundred and twenty minutes, and no mismatches.

The application also accepts a non-sensitive JSON dataset and produces a downloadable report. A separate live check verified four rows and forty-four minutes. Those are different jobs.

An active worker is protected: the labelled safety lab escalates without reclaiming it. An earlier model run exposed a sequencing defect; after the tool catalog was corrected, a later run resumed and verified its report. neatlogs makes those choices inspectable.

Entire links coding context to build checkpoints and commits. In cfo.ai, we modeled a forty-nine-dollar recovery price, inference and infrastructure assumptions, and a higher-price scenario. There are no actual customers or revenue behind that forecast.

Nightshift's first intended users are small teams with application-owned report pipelines. Inspect. Repair. Verify. The prototype and build record are in the public repository.

## Visual direction for review

Concepts considered:

1. **Resume at the checkpoint** — one interrupted job carries its retained progress through legal repair to verified output. Selected because it shows Nightshift's specific behavior and gives the motion a concrete job to do.
2. **Take the night shift** — an unattended-operations mood built around the moon mark. Rejected as the primary story because atmosphere could imply broader production autonomy than the prototype supports.
3. **Recovery with receipts** — a forensic before/after story with the result as the hero. Retained as the payoff, rather than making the whole film an evidence presentation.

Owner selected **white and cool blue** after viewing the graphite/yellow proposal. Cold white (`#F4F8FC`), dark blue type (`#16334A`), pale blue surfaces (`#E2EEF7`) and steel-blue action accents (`#2F789F`) form the film stage. Product/source evidence keeps its actual colors. Typography, macro results and a single job ticket carry the launch; no paper-invoice motif or long screenshot walkthrough.

Three Remotion style frames cover the opening, animated product explanation and result proof. These establish look and framing before another full render. The illustrated demo follows actual stored observations and is labelled accordingly; no fictional interface or fabricated execution is presented as live footage.

Technique draw: launch/high, seed `498928029`. Retain shape matches, an isolating camera push, considered depth, concise unit labels and the callback ending. Drop a multi-job pile-up because the bounded first build has one controlled recovery; drop glitch/dash-burst decoration and specular effects that do not help explain the repair. Actor animation cannot imply measured runtime speed.
